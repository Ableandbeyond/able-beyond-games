The Co-Creation Process

How we collaborate with schools and families to build the perfect targeted intervention.

1

Consultation

We sit down with the teacher, SENCO, or family to review the student's specific IEP/EHCP targets, sensory needs, and special interests.

2

Rapid Prototyping

We build a bespoke browser game tailored to the student's exact cognitive, sensory, and accessibility profile using clean, modern code.

3

Pilot & Track

We deploy the game on a secure URL for a 2-week testing window, allowing educators to monitor engagement and track progress against IEP targets.
